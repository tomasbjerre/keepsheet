KeepSheet turns paper and photos into clean, searchable PDFs, and lets you merge existing PDFs into one, in whatever order you choose. Everything happens on your device — no accounts, no cloud.

WHAT IT DOES
- Scan pages with your camera, or import existing photos — KeepSheet automatically detects the page edges and corrects the perspective so it looks scanned, not photographed at an angle.
- Capture several pages in a row, reorder or retake any of them, and save them as one PDF.
- Apply a document filter — color, grayscale, or black-and-white — to make text crisp and shrink file size.
- Recognize text on every page (on-device OCR), so your PDFs are searchable later.
- Get a sensible file name suggested automatically for every document.
- Merge several existing PDF files into one, choosing the exact order.
- Browse your document history, search it by name or content, and share any PDF straight from the app.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO
KeepSheet is deliberately simple. There's no annotation or signing, no cloud sync, and no accounts. Your documents stay on your device.

PERMISSIONS
KeepSheet asks for camera access only when you start scanning, and photo/media access only when you choose to import. All processing — cropping, filtering, and text recognition — runs entirely on your device.

KeepSheet is open source. Feedback, bug reports, and feature requests are welcome at the project's GitHub issues page.
